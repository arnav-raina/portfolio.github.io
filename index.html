About

I grew up watching my dad discuss markets and wondering why school textbooks could not explain why markets actually move. That gap between clean theory and messy reality drew me to questions at the intersection of technology, economics, data, and culture: why a tweet can move a stock price, how algorithms shape what people see, and how data influences policy.

I’m a recent school leaver from Delhi with experience in content strategy, editorial work, and social media. At OPA The Periodical and VertX India, I learned to communicate complex ideas clearly and think strategically about how information spreads. Leadership roles at Basava International School and BISMUN’24 strengthened my stakeholder communication, structured decision-making, and negotiation skills. I’m now looking forward to college and building the quantitative and analytical foundation to match my curiosity.

I’m especially interested in fintech, data, AI, and economic research, and would love to connect with people working on interesting ideas in these areas.
